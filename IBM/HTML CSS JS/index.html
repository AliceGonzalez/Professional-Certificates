Unit Conversions

Temperature




To convert to celsius to fahrenheit yourself, use this formula replacing the 'C' with your temperature in celsius" (C x 9/5) + 32
Weight




To convert kilograms to pound yourself, use this formula replacing th 'kg' with your weight in kilograms : kg x 2.205
Distance




To convert kilometers to miles yourself, use this formula replacing the 'km' with your distance in kilometers: km ÷ 1.609
